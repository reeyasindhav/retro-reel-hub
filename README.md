Design Ideas: Retro VHS-inspired aesthetic, decade-based browsing, film grain texture UI, director profile pages, classic movie club feature.

Problem Statement:

Modern streaming platforms cater primarily to mainstream, modern content, making it frustrating and time-consuming for vintage cinema enthusiasts to discover, stream, and discuss classic and cult films. Most existing services lack dedicated curation, rich historical context like director filmographies, and interactive community spaces for movie buffs. 

Retrowave addresses this gap by offering a focused platform that combines curated decade-based browsing, a nostalgic retro-inspired viewing experience, and dedicated community spaces like classic movie clubs to bring vintage cinema back into the spotlight.
